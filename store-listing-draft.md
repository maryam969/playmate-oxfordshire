## App Store (Apple)

**App Name** (30 char max)
```
OxSporties
```

**Subtitle** (30 char max — shows under the name in search results)
```
Play Sport in Oxfordshire
```

**Promotional Text** (170 char max — can be updated anytime without a new build)
```
Find pickup games, book venues, and connect with players across
Oxfordshire. Football, tennis, padel, basketball, and badminton —
all in one app.
```

**Keywords** (100 char max, comma-separated, no spaces after commas)
```
sports,football,tennis,padel,badminton,basketball,oxford,oxfordshire,pickup games,find players,venue
```

**Description** (4000 char max)
```
OxSporties is Oxford's #1 sports platform — helping players find
games, book venues, and connect with a local sports community across
Oxfordshire.

Whether you're new to the city or just want to play more often,
OxSporties makes it easy to find people to play with, right when
you want to play.

FIND A GAME
Browse upcoming games near you across football, tennis, basketball,
badminton, and padel. Filter by sport, venue, and skill level to
find a game that fits.

HOST YOUR OWN
Can't find a game? Create one. Set the venue, time, and spots
available, and let other OxSporties players join in.

GROUP CHAT
Every sport has its own group chat, so you can coordinate with
other players, ask questions, and organise games together.

DISCOVER VENUES
Explore venues across Oxfordshire, from council pitches to private
courts, with everything you need to book and show up.

STAY IN THE LOOP
Get notified when someone joins your game, when a spot opens up in
one you want to join, or when there's a new message in your group.

FREE TO JOIN
OxSporties is free for players. No subscriptions, no hidden fees —
just sport.

Join over 3,800 players already using OxSporties to stay active and
meet new people across Oxford.
```

**Category:** Sports (Primary) — consider Social Networking as secondary if allowed

**Age Rating:** 4+ (unless in-app chat requires a higher rating — Apple
often bumps apps with unmoderated user-to-user chat to 12+ or 17+, so
answer the chat/UGC questions in the rating questionnaire carefully)
