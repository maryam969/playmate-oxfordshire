## 2. Devices tested on
Tested on a physical iPhone (iPhone 17 Pro Max class hardware) running
the latest iOS, and on an Android emulator (Pixel 9, latest Android).

## 3. App description, target audience, value
OxSporties is a hyperlocal sports platform for Oxfordshire, UK. It
helps players find pickup games, book venues, and connect with a local
sports community across football, tennis, basketball, badminton, and
padel. Users can browse and join existing games, host their own, chat
with other players in sport-specific group chats, and get notified
about game updates. The target audience is casual and regular sports
players in and around Oxford who want an easier way to find people to
play with.

## 4. Setup / login instructions
Sign-in is required to access most features. A demo account is
provided in the Sign-In Information section above. Users can also
create their own account via email/password or Google Sign-In. No
sample files are needed — the demo account already has access to
existing games and groups to explore.

## 5. External services used
- Supabase — database, authentication, and backend infrastructure
- Google — Google Sign-In, and Google Maps/geocoding for venue locations
- Firebase Cloud Messaging — push notifications
- Resend — transactional email (e.g. game join notifications)
- Vercel — hosting

## 6. Regional differences
The app functions consistently across all regions/devices. Content
(games, venues, groups) is centered on Oxfordshire, UK, since that is
the platform's local focus, but the app itself has no region-gated
features or region-specific UI differences.

## 7. Regulated industry / protected material
Not applicable — OxSporties does not operate in a regulated industry
and does not include any protected third-party material.
